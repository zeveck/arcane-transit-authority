# Brief: Arcane Transit Authority (ATA)

**In one sentence:** As Chief Engineer of the Arcane Transit Authority, you run the trains of Oikumene, a single planet that is one continuous city, threading elemental trains along glowing ley-rails over, under and straight through it (sky, sea, rock, molten core and orbit) to keep the planet's flow of people and goods pouring toward the Homeworld at the centre of the galaxy, a place you only ever see as a light in the sky.

**The feeling:** The pleasure of a tidy, humming machine; "look at it go". A gleaming neon-and-chrome world with a cyberpunk edge, playful rather than grim. You are the clever engineer who finds the impossible shortcut (straight through the core!) and then makes the city a bit greener while you're at it, on a planet you come to know like a home city.

## Who, where, how long

- Players who enjoy building and management games (transit, city and tycoon games) and want something they can grasp quickly and that looks spectacular.
- Desktop (mouse and keyboard) and phones/tablets (touch), in the browser.
- One game = one **term** as Chief Engineer on the one planet, about 30 to 45 minutes (savable and resumable mid-term). The planet's geography is the same every game; each term varies the starting region, district demand, priority districts, inherited lines, milestones, disasters and dispatches. Each Charter has a mandate that changes what you build first. Between games: spend Merits and pick the next Homeworld Charter (six difficulty tiers) and starting region. Many terms over time.

## Style references

- **"Sim Train" / railroad sims (A-Train, Railroad Tycoon, transit puzzlers like Mini Metro).** Borrow: connecting places to create demand, money from ridership, the satisfaction of watching a network work. Avoid: spreadsheet-heavy micromanagement, and any of their names, UI or art.
- **Futurama's pneumatic tubes** (from the request, *loose*). Borrow only: long articulated trains threading a dense retro-future city on lines of light. Avoid: enclosing tubes, and any characters, logos, names or exact designs from the show.
- **Ley lines** (folklore: lines of power across a land). Borrow: the idea of glowing lines of power criss-crossing a world, which the ATA's ley-rails make literal.
- **"A city Robotnik would love."** Borrow: the joyful excess of a planet-wide industrial machine city, chrome and smokestacks and lights. Avoid: any character, badge or level art from Sonic games.
- **Ecumenopolis (planet-wide city, as in classic SF).** Borrow: the idea, and the view of a whole globe glittering with lights at night.

## Settings and decisions made on their behalf

- 3D web game (three.js, vendored), desktop and phone, large scope, $20 budget, 2 review rounds, plan only, design shared as a page with $10 of mock pictures.
- Name: **Arcane Transit Authority** ("ATA" large on the logo; "Arcane Transit" as the app's short name). "Ecumenopolis" is the word for the planet-city.
- One planet, **Oikumene**, with fixed geography: six starting regions (the Capital, the Brine, the Spires, the Rift, the Flats, the Ringside) and the Far Wards around the Warp Terminal. The Homeworld is never visited.
- Every line is a **ley-rail** in its train type's colour, and trains carry their own element's protection (a water bubble, wind ribbons, lightning, lava wake) as they travel straight through sky, sea, rock and magma.
- Building routes: tap two stations and pick a train type; the game works out the path and its cost (no free-hand 3D track laying).
- Each game is one term under a **Homeworld Charter**; its "job well done" is reaching the Charter's throughput quota sent to the Homeworld through the deep space warp line. Each Charter has a signature mandate (for example, Atlantis exports doubled, or no new demolition in the Capital). Commending a Charter unlocks the next Charter and a new starting region; Commending Charter VI plays the finale. Good terms earn **Merits** to spend between games on unlocks.
