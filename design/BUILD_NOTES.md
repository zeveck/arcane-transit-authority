### Cards and building
- Card **cost includes the 2 trains per new line** (the design's Balance table quotes costs without trains). Surface stations demolish their own cell (station = 150 + that cell's demolition), so a Surface card demolishes every path cell including its new station cells: the tutorial's first card is $2,621 with trains ($2,021 without), 5 blocks (design example $1,780, 4 blocks). Still the Cheapest card.
- **Shafts have no track cost** (they are in the Core Exchange price), which matches the Balance table's core crossing ~$4,700 + trains.
- The Warp Terminal's surface and earth layers are free (already built); a Space Elevator there costs the elevator.
- A Sky Port is always its own station on the peak (walkway-linked), never a layer added to a ground station; ground stations take surface/earth/space layers; Sea Stations only water.
- Sky range = great-circle distance between the peaks at radius 100. Sky arc h gets +3 per existing sky line with an end within 1 cell of either new end.
- **Space capacity** follows the line formula (2 trains ≈ 150/min for 150° of ring); the design's "~350" is not reachable with 6×30 cars and the formula, so the formula wins.
- Multi-speed lines (space with elevators, Rift shafts) use round trip = 2 × Σ(segment / speed) + 8 s.
- Walkways: boarding at a walkway neighbour of the origin, or arriving through one, counts as one transfer at the station entered. Pairs of walkway-linked stations generate no commuter demand (they walk).
- Components use every built line (demand persists while a line is broken: riders wait); paths use only open lines.
- Card "carries" counts interior transfers only (Core Exchanges, the Hybrid's via station), not the end walkways.
- Inherited network: count (2–4) and lines (1–2) come from the sub-stream hash(term seed, 'term', 'inherited'); cells within 2 of the Terminal are skipped; a listed cell without a station within 1 cell gets a free Bore station.

### Disasters, undo, determinism
- Brace prices = 50% of the repair price × Charter price; Solar Storm and Warp Drift have no repair, so they brace on a $300 base. Braced Meteor = 1 segment fewer (no further halving); braced Blackout = radius 1, same 45 s. Riders lost when a line breaks unbraced = the line's flow × its trip time (removed proportionally from the in-flight buckets).
- Warp Drift is only eligible once something has launched; Blackouts target stations that have a line.
- `hash()` excludes the action log, so the same state hashes the same however reached. Solutions record the quota hash right after the quota-meeting tick, before any action at that second (browser replay: check before acting).
- Line flows (EMA) and the delivered/reaching/fare EMAs live in the state because disasters and milestones read them.

### Engine independence (reply to "Request to the sim (3)")
- **Root cause:** cell positions were rebuilt at run time with `Math.sin/cos/atan2`, and several sim decisions compare trig-derived floats with thresholds (priority districts ≥ 45°, earth max angle, core ≥ 60°, long-haul > 90°, sky range, ring reach), so a last-bit difference between engines could flip a decision.
- **Fix:** (a) the grid's positions (rounded to 1e-12) and neighbour lists are frozen in `tuning.js` as `OIKUMENE_GRID` and the game never rebuilds them (corners for drawing are computed from them with arithmetic and `sqrt` only, which IEEE makes exact everywhere). (b) Every trig-derived scalar is quantised at its source: `angleRad` 1e-12, `angleDeg` / `gcDist` / lat-lon / `ringTrackDeg` / max earth angle 1e-9, line and segment lengths 1e-6 (they feed capacity, trip time, cost). With frozen inputs the remaining arithmetic (+ − × ÷ sqrt) is correctly rounded in every engine.
